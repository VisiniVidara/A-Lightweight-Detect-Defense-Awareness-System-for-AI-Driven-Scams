    A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

    Good questions.
    Clear answers.

    Understand the setup before you start.

    Check download availability ↗
    Does MailSentra work in my web browser?

    The website introduces the product and distributes the installer. MailSentra analyzes email through its Thunderbird extension and supporting services. It is not currently a Gmail or Outlook browser extension.

    Why do I need a pairing key?

    Your secret pairing key connects your installation to your registration. It appears on screen after your subscription payment is verified. No pairing email is sent. Enter it only in the trusted MailSentra setup flow.

    What will happen to a suspicious email?

    The system can allow, restrict, quarantine or block a message according to its findings and configured permissions. Review your installed version’s settings and setup guide before enabling protective actions, particularly any permanent removal.

    Can MailSentra prove an email was written by AI?

    No. Possible AI-writing signals are supporting information. They are separate from scam risk, and AI-written text alone does not establish that a message is a scam.

    Does every image get scanned?

    The current scope is PNG attachments and LSB steganography detection. A heatmap shows regions that influenced a prediction; it does not extract a payload or prove malicious intent.

    Is there a subscription or a price?

    MailSentra is US$20 per month, billed in USD. The first payment is due at checkout, with monthly renewals until cancelled. Cancel future renewals using your private subscription access code in the subscription panel. Live checkout opens only after merchant approval and hosting configuration; refunds and support details must be published before launch.

    What if a check cannot finish?

    An incomplete analysis should be treated as unverified. The demo shows this explicitly instead of presenting the email as safe. Follow the explanation and recheck before interacting with attachments.
