Enterprise-Grade Architecture

Designed for High-Assurance Email Defense

By dismantling the technical silos between NLP threat categorization and computer vision steganalysis, MailSentra neutralizes attacks that conventional secure email gateways cannot see.

✉

NLP Linguistic Triage (C1)

Fine-tuned DistilBERT transformer models coupled with SPF/DKIM authentication verification, 226k+ malicious URL threat intel feeds, and stylometric AI text profiling.

👁

Visual Steganalysis & Grad-CAM (C3)

Deep convolutional neural networks (EfficientNet-B2) detect data hidden across pixel arrays, generating localized visual heatmaps to confirm payload carrier integrity.

⚖

Declarative Defense Policy (C2)

18-rule declarative decision engine mapping multi-modal evidence into calibrated postures (Informational to High Protection) without volatile neural fusion.

🛡

Never-Fail-Open Guarantee

Zero speculative clean ratings. If attachment processing crashes or times out, the platform issues an explicit partial status with active warnings.

🎓

Evidence-Grounded Awareness

Generates contextual, plain-language explanations of threat mechanisms at the moment of reading, while tracking longitudinal employee vulnerability progression over time.

🔌

Native Mail Client Extension

Seamlessly integrated with Mozilla Thunderbird WebExtension standard, delivering immediate warnings and interactive Grad-CAM inspection panels in the user's workflow.
