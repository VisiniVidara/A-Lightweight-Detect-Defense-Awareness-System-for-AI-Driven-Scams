## C. Main Content Sections

### 7.1 Verified Communication Guidelines
In accordance with academic privacy standards, personal mobile phone numbers and unverified student email addresses are withheld from public indexing until institutional sign-off.

- **Primary Communication Channel:** Official Research Project Email / Academic Department Inquiries
- **Physical Lab / Faculty Address:** `NOT AVAILABLE` (Placeholder: Department of Computing Sciences, University Research Complex)
- **Official Research Group Email:** `NOT AVAILABLE` (Placeholder: `research-MailSentra@institution.ac.lk` or `project-inquiries@MailSentra-research.org`)
- **Supervisor Office Contact:** `NOT AVAILABLE` (Placeholder: Available upon formal academic request)
- **Repository & Code Inquiries:** Contact via designated faculty repository coordinator.

---

### 7.2 Academic Inquiry Form & Message Templates

To facilitate structured communication with examiners and researchers, the following standard inquiry categories and email templates are provided:

#### 1. Examiner & Evaluation Inquiries
- **Subject Line:** `[MailSentra Evaluation] Academic Examination Query - [Institution/Examiner Name]`
- **Template Body:**
  ```text
  Dear MailSentra Research Team,

  I am writing to inquire regarding the MailSentra Multi-Modal Email Security Platform presented for academic evaluation.

  Specifically, I would like to request clarification on:
  [ ] Live demonstration reproduction steps (Scenario 1–8)
  [ ] Access to held-out evaluation corpora and testing datasets
  [ ] Clarification on the Component 02 policy engine rulebook (v1.5.0)
  [ ] Component 03 steganalysis training methodologies and Grad-CAM implementation

  Please provide relevant academic documentation at your earliest convenience.

  Sincerely,
  [Examiner / Reviewer Name]
  [Academic Affiliation / Title]
  ```

#### 2. Research Collaboration & Data Access Inquiries
- **Subject Line:** `[MailSentra Research] Collaborative Inquiry - [University/Lab Name]`
- **Template Body:**
  ```text
  Dear Research Team and Supervisors,

  We are reviewing your research on multi-modal email security and policy-driven defense mechanisms. We are interested in exploring potential research collaborations or referencing your evidence base.

  Topic of Interest:
  [ ] Multi-modal evidence normalization contracts (SecurityAssessmentEnvelope)
  [ ] Longitudinal vulnerability profiling in email clients
  [ ] Comparative analysis of image steganalysis in enterprise mail streams

  We look forward to connecting with your project supervisors.

  Kind regards,
  [Researcher Name]
  [Research Group / Institution]
  ```
