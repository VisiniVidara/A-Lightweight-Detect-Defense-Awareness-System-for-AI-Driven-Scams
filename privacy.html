MAILSENTRA / CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Early-access requests

With your consent, the service stores your name, email, phone number, consent version and submission time in a private database to manage your request and follow up about product access. It does not enroll you in unrelated marketing. Requests are retained for the configured period, 30 days by default, and duplicate emails do not create another record. Billing address and card information are not collected for early access.

Registration and payment

The website stores username, email, phone, consent version and timestamps, pairing status and installation ID. Billing name and address are submitted through the browser to PayHere together with email, phone and order information for checkout. PayHere handles card details; this website does not collect or store card numbers or CVV.

Access and storage

Subscription references, payment status, dates and paid entitlement are stored in a private SQLite database. Access codes, keys, download tokens and installation sessions are stored as keyed hashes. The raw pairing key is displayed on screen and is not saved in browser storage. A subscription management code is saved in sessionStorage for the current browser session; anyone with that code can manage the subscription. No pairing email is sent and email addresses are not verified.

Retention and contact

Billing-linked records are retained for subscription operation; unlinked registrations are removed after the configured retention period, defaulting to 30 days. Before live launch the operator must publish its identity, privacy contact, retention schedule and process for access/deletion requests. No analytics or advertising trackers are included. Google Fonts receives network information when serving fonts.

Email content

The website demonstration uses preset examples and does not upload email content. Data handling in the installed product is separate and must be explained in its installation documentation.
