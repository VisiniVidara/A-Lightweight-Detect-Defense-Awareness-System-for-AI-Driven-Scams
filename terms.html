MAILSENTRA / CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Subscription terms

Early-access requests

Early access is an expression of interest, not a purchase, paid subscription or guaranteed release date. We save your name, email and phone number with your consent so the MailSentra team can follow up about your request. No payment, pairing key or installer is provided by this form. No confirmation email is sent automatically.

Monthly subscription

The proposed MailSentra plan costs US$20 per month, charged now and automatically each month until cancellation. Checkout is unavailable until the merchant account, installation package and commercial launch requirements are complete. Sandbox checkout is for testing only.

Payment and cancellation

Payments are handled on PayHere’s hosted page. Save your private subscription management code to check payment and cancel automatic renewal from this website. Cancellation preserves the current paid period and does not itself issue a refund. Final seller identity, support, refund, tax and software-license terms must be published before live sales.

Access

The Thunderbird installation package is free to download without registration or payment. Activation requires a pairing key issued after server-verified payment. Keys are single-use and expire within 24 hours or at the end of the paid period. The public installer link does not expire; temporary links issued alongside activation expire within 30 minutes. Email addresses are not verified and keys are not emailed. The installed product must integrate subscription verification. Keep all access codes and keys private.

Product limitations

MailSentra requires the documented Thunderbird and backend environment. Detection is an assessment, not a guarantee. Website examples are simulations. Review protective settings before enabling quarantine or deletion.
